# Studio Photography Constraints

Image generation prompts dynamically inject constraints to guarantee commercial photography compliance.

---

## Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Color Format** | `RGB` | Web display profile |
| **Bit Depth** | `8 bits per pixel` | Standard 24-bit RGB space |
| **Pixel Dimensions** | `2200px x 2200px` | 2K high-resolution square rendering |
| **Aspect Ratio** | `1:1 (Square)` | Uniform grid alignment |
| **Background Color** | `Seamless White (255, 255, 255 RGB)` | Zero gradients, shadows, or floor occlusions |

---

## Strict Negative Guidelines (DON'Ts)

- **No Safety Violations**: Zero violence, hate, discrimination, nudity, vulgarity, or obscenity.
- **No Text Overlays**: Zero promotional claims, sale banners, warranty stickers, or marketing badges.
- **No Retailer Branding**: Zero retailer logos, watermarks, or third-party platform icons.
- **No Unincluded Accessories**: Zero extraneous props, background furniture, or accessories not included in the SKU packaging.
- **No Out-of-Stock Indicators**: No inventory status markings.
- **No Condition Descriptors**: No "Refurbished", "Used", or "Open Box" labels.
- **No Non-English Text**: All typographic elements must be English.
- **No Collages or Split Views**: Single unified frame showing only the primary subject.

---

## Required Positive Guidelines (DOs)

- **Accurate Identity Matching**: Faithfully reproduce the physical product described in catalog metadata.
- **Studio Lighting**: Sharp focus, professional diffuse studio lighting, and high dynamic range.
- **Photographic Realism**: Realistic textures, natural reflections, and tangible physical materials (not CGI/3D render artifacts).
- **Close Cropping**: Subject fills the frame with balanced margins.
- **Front-Facing Perspective**: Primary image must display the primary front face of the product.

---

## Fidelity & Detail Adherence

- **Strict Feature Counts**: If a product is described with two buttons, three stripes, or four USB ports, the generated asset must render exactly that count.
- **Typography Precision**: Render branding text exactly as specified in orientation, case, font family, and placement without hallucinations.
- **Absolute Pure White Canvas**: The product must appear isolated cleanly on RGB `(255, 255, 255)` without floor contact shadows, reflections, or atmospheric haze.
